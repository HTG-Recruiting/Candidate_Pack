How to prepare

Four things that help

  1. 01

    Revisit the role

    Re-read the job ad. Pick the two or three outcomes that matter most and how you’d tackle them.

  2. 02

    Bring real examples

    A few stories from your work: the situation, what you did, what changed. Specific beats polished.

  3. 03

    Try what we build

    Plan a trip on HomeToGo or browse the app. Note one thing you liked and one you’d change.

  4. 04

    Bring your questions

    The interview runs both ways. Ask about the team, the work and how success is measured.
